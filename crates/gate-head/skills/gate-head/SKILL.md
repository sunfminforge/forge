name: gate-head
description: Use when a review needs to know whether guild gate is green on exactly the worktree's HEAD tree, with nothing uncommitted: one call runs guild gate once and prints its output, then the head commit, its tree, the tree the gate names, and whether they match.
---

Run `gate-head --help` before the first run; the help is the whole manual.
Through Guild: `guild tool gate-head [--slow] [--remote <name>]`.
